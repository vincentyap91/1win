Service rules

General rules
Betwave is a free platform where you can create and share sports Predictions. Key terms and concepts: Prediction — a virtual bet on a sporting event published on the service and containing the author’s opinion on the match outcome. Predictor — any registered Betwave user who creates Predictions. General platform rules: All Predictions are settled according to 1win rules. Odds, payout and refund conditions, as well as the details of individual markets, follow the platform’s General Rules and Conditions. If a Prediction includes bets on events that are cancelled, postponed, or do not take place, those bets will be settled at odds of 1.0.
Top predictors contest rules

This is a monthly competition for Predictors. Winners are determined by the highest ROI (Return on Investment) achieved from the Predictions*. You can see your ROI in the Profit tab in your Betwave profile. To participate, publish 5 Predictions. There are no extra requirements or restrictions.

ROI (Return on Investment) is a measure of betting performance. It is calculated by dividing your profit by the total amount wagered and multiplying the result by 100%. For example, if you place bets totaling 1,000 vUSD and earn 150 vUSD in profit, your ROI is 15%. This means that the size of your balance is not what matters most—it's the accuracy of your Predictions

Results are summed up on the last day of each calendar month. Only settled bets are counted — pending bets do not affect the results. Cash prizes are awarded to the 20 Predictors with the highest ROI. The prize pool is distributed as follows.

Requirements for Predictions and Fair Play

Only independent predictions count toward the minimum participation requirement.

The following will NOT be counted toward the minimum number of predictions:

  • Posting multiple identical predictions for the same event and outcome.
  • Reposting the same prediction to satisfy the minimum participation requirement.
  • Mass posting of identical or substantially similar predictions.
  • Any actions intended to artificially satisfy the participation requirements or obtain an unfair competitive advantage.

The Organizer reserves the right to treat multiple identical predictions as a single prediction or exclude them from the competition results.

If abuse of the competition mechanics is detected, including spam, automated posting, duplicate predictions, or any other attempt to circumvent the rules, the Organizer reserves the right to disqualify the participant and void their results without awarding prizes.

* Starting June 9, 2026, winners are determined by ROI instead of profit. Top Predictors has been updated to reflect the new rules.

Prediction creation rules
You can post Predictions only for Pre-match events. Live events are not available for creating Predictions. A Prediction can consist of single or multiple bets. The maximum number of events in a Prediction is 3. The minimum bet amount is 1% of your Betwave virtual balance. The maximum bet amount is 10%. You can create an unlimited number of Predictions, even for the same event. Once a Prediction is posted, it cannot be edited or deleted. The Prediction description must not contain profanity, random strings of numbers, symbols, letters, or words. You cannot publish images, videos, or audio, and writing in Caps Lock is not allowed. Make sure your Prediction does not contain links or any advertising. Repeated violations may result in your account being banned.
