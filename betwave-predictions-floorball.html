FAQ

What is Betwave?
Betwave is a free platform where you can create sports predictions, share them with friends, and discuss betting results. Rise to the top of the predictions leaderboard and win valuable prizes from 1win.
How do I post a prediction?
Go to the Betwave section in the sidebar. Click Create Prediction, choose the match you’re interested in and its outcome. Then enter your bet and share your thoughts on the match outcome in the description. The description must not contain profanity or links. When you’re done, click Post Prediction so others can see it.
Can I withdraw my winnings?
Bets on Betwave are placed using a virtual bankroll. It is not real money, so winnings cannot be withdrawn. To win real money, place a real-money bet by selecting a prediction you like and clicking Bet.
How do I become a top predictor?
As soon as you post 5 predictions, you are included in the ranking. The data is updated regularly, and results are summarized once a month.
How can I make money from predictions?
Post predictions, improve their accuracy, increase your profit from winning bets, and move up the predictor rankings. The best participants receive valuable prizes from 1win. Explore predictions from other authors, subscribe to them, repeat bets, and place your own on 1win.
How do I find the most accurate prediction?
The most popular predictions are featured on the Betwave home page. For deeper analysis, visit predictors’ profiles to review their prediction history, betting profitability, and comments on other people's predictions. Follow authors and find their publications in the "Subscriptions" section.
Is there a limit on the number of predictions per match?
No, you can create multiple predictions for the same event.
