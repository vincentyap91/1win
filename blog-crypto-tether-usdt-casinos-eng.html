What Is Tether (USDT)? 

Tether (USDT) is a stablecoin – a cryptocurrency with a fixed value. Each USDT token is pegged to the US dollar, so 1 USDT should always equal 1 dollar. This stability comes from reserves held by Tether Limited.

Launched in 2014, Tether was one of the first stablecoins. Today, it ranks among the top cryptocurrencies by market capitalisation and trading volume. For online gambling, USDT offers fast, private crypto transactions.

How Tether (USDT) Works

Tether uses a reserve-based model. Tether Limited claims each USDT in circulation has an equivalent asset in reserve – mainly cash, cash equivalents, and other financial instruments.

When users buy USDT on an exchange, new tokens may be issued. Exchanging USDT for fiat burns tokens. This maintains the 1:1 peg and stable value.

USDT runs on several blockchains. The same token can be transferred via Ethereum, TRON, BNB Chain, and other networks. Each functions identically in value, but speed and fees differ.
