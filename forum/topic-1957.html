    Italian GP Qualifying — The Grid Nobody Saw Coming at Monza

    Monza usually rewards raw power unit strength, and the pole time genuinely surprised most people watching.
    None of the usual favorites for this track made the front row.

    Russell and Piastri came closest to matching the top time, within two tenths.

    ⚡ What Stands Out

    Leclerc and Hamilton starting 4th and 5th, right in front of Verstappen in 6th
    Antonelli right behind them in 7th for Mercedes, and Colapinto making Q3 for the same team that took pole, in 8th
    Norris only 9th, a surprising gap from his teammate Piastri sitting on the front row
    Sainz struggling to 15th on Williams, and Cadillac's Bottas and Pérez rounding out the bottom of the top 20 in their debut season
    Alonso and Stroll at the very back of the grid, 21st and 22nd

    Who are you actually backing to win Sunday's race? 👇
